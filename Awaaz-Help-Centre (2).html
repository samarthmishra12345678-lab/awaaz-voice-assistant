🚀 Getting started

Set up takes about five minutes. There is no account and no password.

1. Install it to the home screen

Open Awaaz in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) and choose Add to Home Screen. A 🎙️ icon appears next to your other apps.

2. Allow the microphone

The first time you press 🎙️ the phone asks "Allow microphone?" — tap Allow. Nothing works without this; listening is the whole idea.

3. Allow notifications

Tap Allow when asked. This is what lets a medicine reminder reach you.

4. Press and speak

Press 🎙️, wait for "Sun rahi hoon…", then speak normally. Try: Subah 8 baje dawai yaad dilao

5. Add a caregiver

Say caregiver number 98765 43210. Now an unanswered reminder can reach a real person.

That is the whole setup. One button, and you are done.

🚨 Missed-dose escalation

The feature that makes Awaaz more than a reminder app.

  1. A reminder fires — notification, sound, vibration, and a spoken alert twice.
  2. A confirmation window opens — 3 minutes.
  3. If nobody taps Theek hai, a loud banner appears: "Dawai confirm nahi hui".
  4. One tap calls the caregiver, or opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message.
  5. Confirming at any point cancels it.
Why this exists: an unconfirmed dose is the one that matters. Every other health app treats "we notified them" as success. Awaaz treats it as unfinished.

Add a caregiver first: caregiver number 98765 43210 — or tap + Caregiver number jodo.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

"I press the button and nothing happens"

  • Microphone denied — Browser settings → Site settings → Microphone → Allow, then reload.
  • Needs a reload — close the tab and reopen from the home-screen icon.

"It does not understand me"

  1. Wait for the prompt. Press 🎙️, wait for "Sun rahi hoon…", then speak. Speaking too early is the most common cause.
  2. Speak a little slower — but normally. Do not shout.
  3. Turn the TV or fan down.
  4. Check the language buttons at the top.
  5. Just type it. Every command works by typing too.

"The reminder did not ring"

SituationWhat happens
App open or minimisedReminders always fire
App fully closedAny missed reminder fires the moment you reopen Awaaz
Phone was off for daysDaily reminders reschedule themselves
Phone on silentIn-app alarm, vibration and spoken alert still fire

"It speaks but I cannot hear it"

  • Raise the media volume (not the ringer).
  • Disconnect Bluetooth, or select the phone speaker.
  • Tap the 🔊 Suno button — browsers only allow speech after you touch something.

"I lost all my notes"

Notes live on the phone itself. They are deleted if you clear browser data or use incognito mode. There is no cloud copy — by design.

👨‍👩‍👧 For families & caregivers

Awaaz is used by someone else's parent. This page is for the person setting it up.

10-minute setup checklist

  • Added to home screen, renamed to something recognisable ("Dawai")
  • Microphone permission allowed
  • Notification permission allowed
  • Caregiver number saved
  • Emergency (SOS) number saved
  • One daily medicine reminder set by the user
  • Font size set for their eyes
  • Escalation tested once, together
  • Family told: a missed-dose alert is a nudge, not an emergency

The call you might get

If a reminder goes unanswered for three minutes, the app offers to call or WhatsApp you.

What it means: a reminder fired and nobody confirmed it. What it does not mean: that the medicine was definitely not taken. It is a nudge to check in, not an alarm to panic.

What Awaaz does NOT do

It does not…Because
Work while fully closed and lockedTrue background push needs a push server or native app — on the roadmap
Detect falls or health eventsIt is a voice assistant, not a medical device
Replace a caregiverIt reduces what a caregiver has to remember
Sync between phonesEverything stays on one device, by design
Guarantee speech accuracyAccents and noisy rooms affect it — hence the typing fallback
Awaaz lowers the effort of asking for help from "find the app, unlock it, navigate, type" to "press once and speak". It does not make an elderly person independent — it makes them less dependent on being near you at the exact moment they need something.

♿ Accessibility

Not a feature added at the end — for this audience it is the product.

Voice-first Nothing requires reading, typing or precise tapping.
Spoken output Every reply is read aloud, not just displayed.
ARIA labels On every control, in all three languages.
Live region aria-live="polite" announces each reply.
Skip-to-mic link One keystroke to the primary control.
Font scaling By voice or button, and it persists.
High-contrast dark mode By voice or button.
Huge tap targets No double-taps, no long-press, no gestures.
Type-to-command The same router, for when speech fails.

Because Awaaz speaks its own replies, it avoids competing with NVDA or TalkBack — the user does not need a screen reader running at all.

🔒 Privacy

No account No login, no email, no phone verification.
No server No database, no analytics, no telemetry.
No cloud voice No cloud processing of speech.
Device-only storage Everything lives in the phone's own localStorage.

The only network request the app ever makes is the city name sent to Open-Meteo when you ask for the weather. Nothing else leaves the device.

WhatsApp and phone links are local intents — they hand off to an app already on the phone. Awaaz sends nothing on your behalf.

⚠️ Known limitations

Stated plainly, because an honest limitation builds more trust than an over-claim.

  • True background push is not implemented. Reminders fire while the app is open or minimised, and missed ones fire on reopen. Firing while fully closed needs a push server or a native wrapper.
  • Speech accuracy depends on the device engine and ambient noise — hence the typing fallback.
  • Reminders are device-local and do not sync between phones.
  • Three languages only — Tamil, Telugu and Bengali are on the roadmap.
